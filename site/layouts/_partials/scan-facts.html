What you get
  • An accessibility sweep of the public pages it finds. It tests them against WCAG 2.2 levels A and AA.
  • Findings from at least one persona, a fictional visitor. Each finding comes with its evidence.
What it is not
  • It is not an audit.
  • It is not a certificate that your site meets WCAG or any law.
  • It samples pages. A clean report does not clear the rest of the site.
