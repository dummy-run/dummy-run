What you get
  • An accessibility check of your public pages against WCAG 2.2 levels A and AA.
  • What a persona, a fictional visitor, ran into while trying your site, with the evidence for each problem so you can check it yourself.
What it is not
  • It isn’t an audit, and it isn’t a certificate that your site meets WCAG or any law.
  • It looks at a sample of pages, so a clean report doesn’t clear the rest of your site.
