{{- /* The three steps of the free scan, shared by the home page and the scan page. */ -}}
  1. Paste a public address Any page on your site that anyone can open without signing in.
  2. We take a look An accessibility check and a persona go through your public pages. They only read, so nothing on your site changes.
  3. Read your private report We email you a private link. Only people you share it with can open it.
