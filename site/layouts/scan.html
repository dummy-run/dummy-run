How it treats your site

  • It loads only the public pages of your site and never signs in.
  • It is read-only. Only GET, HEAD and OPTIONS requests reach your site.
  • It is rate limited, with a cap on pages for each scan.
  • It identifies itself in the user agent and it honours your robots.txt.
  • It stops when you ask. Dummy Run visited my site says how.

The text and screenshots of the pages it visits on your site go to the model that plays the persona. The privacy page covers the waitlist today. Read the privacy page
