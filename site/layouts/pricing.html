Questions about buying

Who is Paddle?
Paddle.com sells the full report and Watch on our behalf as merchant of record. Paddle handles your payment, tax and refunds, its buyer terms apply to those purchases, and we never see your card number. Audits we invoice ourselves. Terms
Can I cancel Watch?
Yes, whenever you like, from your account or the link on your Paddle receipt. You keep access until the end of the month you’ve paid for. We don’t refund part months unless the law requires it. Refund policy
Is the free scan really free?
Yes, it costs nothing. It isn’t open yet{{ if $scan }}, so join the waitlist to hear when it is{{ end }}. Treat it as a useful first look rather than an audit.{{ if $scan }} The free scan{{ end }}
Do you store my site’s content?
The free scan will have its own privacy notice before it opens. For now the privacy page covers this site, the waitlist and purchases. The scan only reads public pages. It doesn’t log in, submit forms or change anything. Privacy

Other questions? Write to hello@dummyrun.dev. The refund policy and terms apply to every purchase.
