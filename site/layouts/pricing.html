Questions about buying

What does “excludes tax” mean?
The price on the card is before tax. For the full report and Watch, Paddle adds any VAT or sales tax due at checkout, based on where you are. Audits are invoiced by Dummy Run directly. We are not registered for VAT, so no VAT is added.
Who is Paddle?
Paddle.com is the merchant of record for paid services. It handles payment, tax and refunds, and its buyer terms apply to the purchase. It takes your payment details. We never see your card number. Terms
Can I cancel Watch?
Yes, any time. Cancel from your account or from the link on your Paddle receipt. Access continues to the end of the paid month. We do not refund a part month except where the law requires it. Refund policy
Is the free scan really free?
Yes. It costs nothing. It is not open yet, so join the waitlist. Its results are for information and are not an audit.{{ if $scan }} The free scan{{ end }}
Do you store my site’s content?
The free scan will get its own privacy notice before it opens. Today the privacy page covers this site, the waitlist and purchases. The scan only reads public pages. It does not log in, submit forms or change anything. Privacy

Other questions? Write to hello@dummyrun.dev. The refund policy and terms apply to every purchase.
