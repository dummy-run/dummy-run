Straight answers

Does it replace our scripted tests?
No. A script checks the steps you wrote. A persona tries the jobs you did not script. Beside your scripted suite
Which browsers does it drive?
Chrome and Firefox, in desktop and phone-sized windows. The browser table
Do screenshots leave our network?
They go to the model provider and the results store you choose. Exported telemetry carries none. What goes where
How do I install it?
Run go install, then point one persona at a site in read-only mode. Get started
