Straight answers

Does it replace our scripted tests?
No, keep them. A script checks the steps you wrote down. A persona wanders off and tries the things nobody thought to script. How they work together
What does it check?
Keyboard use, zoom and bigger text, contrast, plain English, forms, cookie banners and more, on every page it sweeps. Each problem comes with its evidence. Every check
Can a persona replace testing with disabled people?
No. A persona simulates a barrier so you catch likely problems early and cheaply. It can’t tell you how a disabled person would actually get on. Only testing with disabled people can. Ethics and limits
Do screenshots leave our network?
Screenshots go only to the model provider you pick, and the screen-reader persona never sends any. Runs stay in the folder or bucket where you keep them. What goes where

Run one persona today

Install the command, send one persona to a site in read-only mode and read what it found. Read-only mode blocks anything that could change data, apart from signing in with a test account.
