On 19 October 2026 we sent Priya, a QA lead, to dummyrun.dev in Firefox. Her job was to work out what Dummy Run does and how a finding gets checked. Nobody scripted the path. A model chose every click and every scroll.

Priya got there, so the run passed. Along the way she pointed out that the site had browser details scattered through the schema and the examples but no single list of supported browsers, which is the kind of gap a real visitor would notice and we hadn't.
