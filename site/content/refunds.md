Paddle is our merchant of record for the full report and Watch and processes their refunds. We handle audit refunds ourselves. Nothing here removes your statutory rights. If the law gives you a right to a refund, you have it.

## Full report

These terms are in addition to your statutory rights. You can have a full refund within 14 days of purchase if the report has not been generated. If the report failed for a reason on our side you can have a refund or a re-run. Tell us which you prefer.

## Watch

Cancel any time from your account or from the link on your Paddle receipt. Access continues to the end of the paid month. We do not refund a part month except where the law requires it.

## Audits

Refunds follow the order form for your audit. Dummy Run issues them to the original payment method.

## How to ask

For audit refunds, write to hello@dummyrun.dev with your order details. For full report and Watch purchases, ask Paddle through your receipt.
