Last updated: 20 October 2026.

Paddle is our merchant of record and processes every refund. Nothing here removes your statutory rights. If the law gives you a right to a refund, you have it.

## Full report

You can have a full refund within 14 days of purchase if the report has not been generated. If the report failed for a reason on our side you can have a refund or a re-run. Tell us which you prefer.

## Watch

Cancel any time from your account or from the link on your Paddle receipt. Access continues to the end of the paid month. We do not refund a part month except where the law requires it.

## Audits

Refunds follow the order form for your audit.

## How to ask

Write to hello@dummyrun.dev with your order details. You can also ask Paddle through your receipt.
