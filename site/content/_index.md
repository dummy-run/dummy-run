{{< lead >}}Dummy Run signs a fictional user into a preview of your app and lets it try to do its job in Chrome or Firefox. It reports what broke, what confused it and what its role should not have seen. A reviewer checks every claim against screenshots, logs and code before it reaches the results board.{{< /lead >}}
