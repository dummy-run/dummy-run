Dummy Run is open source under the Apache-2.0 licence. It is also the name of the organisation that publishes it. Joel Merrick founded it. His site is [joelmerrick.co.uk](https://joelmerrick.co.uk).

## Why it exists

Barriers in an app are cheaper to fix before release. Dummy Run lets AI personas use a real app read-only to find them early. It does not replace testing with disabled people. [Ethics and limits](../docs/ETHICS.md) explains what a persona can and cannot tell you.

[How it works](how-it-works.md) describes a run from start to finish.

## Get in touch

Email [hello@dummyrun.dev](mailto:hello@dummyrun.dev) with general questions. Report a security problem through the private route in [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md). Do not open a public issue.

Questions and ideas can also go to [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/dummy-run/dummy-run/discussions).

## Contribute

The code, the personas and this site are all in the [repository](https://github.com/dummy-run/dummy-run). [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md) explains how to propose a change.
