Dummy Run is open source under the Apache-2.0 licence, and it's also the name of the small organisation that publishes it. Joel Merrick started it, and you can find more about him at [joelmerrick.co.uk](https://joelmerrick.co.uk).

## Why it exists

A button nobody can reach with a keyboard is a five-minute fix before release and a support queue after it. Dummy Run sends AI personas through a real app, in read-only mode, so you find those problems while they're still cheap. It doesn't replace testing with disabled people, and [Ethics and limits](../../docs/ETHICS.md) is honest about what a persona can and can't tell you.

[How it works](how-it-works.md) follows a run from start to finish.

## Get in touch

Email [hello@dummyrun.dev](mailto:hello@dummyrun.dev) with any question, or bring ideas to [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/dummy-run/dummy-run/discussions). If you've found a security problem, please use the private route in [SECURITY.md](../../SECURITY.md) rather than a public issue.

## Contribute

The code, the personas and this website all live in the [repository](https://github.com/dummy-run/dummy-run), and changes are welcome. [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md) explains how to propose one.
