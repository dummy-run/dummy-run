The likely cause is one of these. Fix it and try again from the [free scan page](../scan.md).

- The email address didn't look right. Check it for typos.
- The agreement box wasn't ticked. We need it before we can store your address.
- The human check didn't pass. Reload the page and try again.
- You or your network tried too many times. Wait an hour and try again.
- The confirmation link had expired or was already used. Links last 48 hours, so sign up again for a fresh one.
- The list isn't taking sign-ups right now. Try again later.

If it keeps failing, email hello@dummyrun.dev and tell us what happened.
