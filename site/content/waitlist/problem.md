Find the likely cause below and try again from the [start page](../start.md).

- The email address did not look right. Check it for typos.
- The agreement box was not ticked. We need it to store your address.
- The human check did not pass. Reload the page and try again.
- You or your network tried too many times. Wait an hour and try again.
- The confirmation link expired or was already used. Links last 48 hours. Sign up again for a new one.
- The list is not taking sign-ups right now. Try again later.

If it keeps failing, write to hello@dummyrun.dev.
