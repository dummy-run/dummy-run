Every site has screen-reader users, phone users and people who have never seen it before. Each persona below brings one way of using a page. You bring the job, such as "renew a loan" or "change a password".

A persona simulates a barrier to find likely problems. It does not replace testing with disabled people. [Ethics and limits](../../docs/ETHICS.md) says more.

## Make one your own

1. Copy the persona's folder from [casts/library](../../casts/library/README.md) into your own cast.
2. Write a scenario for a job in your app.
3. Run it with the command on its card. If you have not installed the command, [get started](start.md) first. The [persona guide](../../docs/PERSONAS.md) has the steps and the settings a persona can use.

Viewer, editor and admin are three personas. Run all three through the same scenario, and any difference in what each can reach is a [permission finding](for/compliance-and-security.md).
