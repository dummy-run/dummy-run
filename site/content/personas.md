Every site has screen-reader users, people on phones and people who have never seen it before. Each persona below brings one way of using a page, and you bring the job, such as "renew a loan" or "change a password". Run the keyboard-only user through your checkout and you'll find out quickly whether a customer without a mouse can actually pay you.

A persona simulates a barrier so you can catch likely problems early. It doesn't replace testing with disabled people, and [Ethics and limits](../../docs/ETHICS.md) explains what it can and can't tell you.

## Make one your own

1. Copy the persona's folder from [casts/library](../../casts/library/README.md) into your own cast.
2. Write a scenario for a job in your app.
3. Run it with the command on its card. If you haven't installed Dummy Run yet, [get started](start.md) first. The [persona guide](../../docs/PERSONAS.md) covers every setting a persona can use.

The viewer, editor and admin work best together. Run all three through the same job, and anything one of them can reach that it shouldn't is a [permission finding](for/compliance-and-security.md).
