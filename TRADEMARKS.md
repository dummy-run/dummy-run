# Trademark policy

The code is open source under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). The **name "Dummy Run"** and the
**Dummy Run logo and mascot** are not covered by that licence. They identify the project and the
services its maintainer offers, so people can tell the original from a fork.

No trademark registration has been filed yet. This policy states how the name and logo may be used
in the meantime and after registration.

## You may, without asking

- Say that your software works with, runs on, or was built from Dummy Run.
- Use the name in articles, talks, tutorials, reviews and comparisons.
- Run an unmodified copy of Dummy Run inside your organisation and call it Dummy Run.
- Use the logo to link to this project.

## Please ask first

- Offering a hosted or managed service with "Dummy Run" in its name or marketing.
- Using the name or logo on merchandise.

## You may not

- Distribute a modified version under the name "Dummy Run", or a name or logo that could be confused
  with it. Forks are welcome. Give yours its own name and say it is based on Dummy Run.
- Suggest that the project or its maintainer endorses your product or service when it does not.
