# Use the menu and footer at large size

Device: desktop. Start at the home page.

## Given

Hana opens the main menu and goes to the footer.

## Goal

"Reach every main section from the menu without losing any link."

## Steps Hana would take

1. Open the main menu. Say whether all items are still there.
2. Open a submenu if there is one.
3. Scroll to the footer and read its links.
4. Check a sticky header or banner does not hide the content.
5. Finish.

## Success looks like

- The menu stayed usable at large size.
- No fixed bar covered the text she was reading.

## What to report

Menu items that vanished or overlapped. Any fixed element that covered content, with its step.
