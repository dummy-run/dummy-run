# Use the sign-up or contact form at large size

Device: desktop. Start at the home page.

## Given

Hana wants to sign up or get in touch. The final submit is blocked, so she stops at the form.

## Goal

"Fill in the form at large size and see every label, hint and error."

## Steps Hana would take

1. Find the form and open it.
2. Check that each label sits next to its field and nothing overlaps.
3. Type into a field and check the text stays readable.
4. Leave one required field empty and see where the error appears. Do not submit.
5. Finish.

## Success looks like

- Labels, fields and errors were all visible without sideways scrolling.
- Buttons were large enough to hit.

## What to report

Any overlap or cut-off text with its step number. Any error that was off screen.
