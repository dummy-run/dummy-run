# Find a fact on a small screen

Device: phone. Start at the home page.

## Given

Eli wants one fact about [the product], for example opening hours or a price, and has about two minutes.

## Goal

"Find [the fact] in as few screens of scrolling as possible, or give up and say where."

## Steps Eli would take

1. Read the first screen. Say whether it tells him what the site is.
2. Open the menu if there is one and say whether it opens.
3. Follow the link that best matches the fact.
4. Scroll to the answer, counting screens.
5. Finish with the count.

## Success looks like

- He found the fact in under six screens of scrolling.
- Nothing needed a pinch or a sideways scroll.

## What to report

The path with step numbers and the scroll count. Any page that was slow or heavy. Any tap target that was hard to hit.
