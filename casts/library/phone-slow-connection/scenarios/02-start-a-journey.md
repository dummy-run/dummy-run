# Start the sign-up or contact journey on a phone

Device: phone. Start at the home page.

## Given

Eli wants to sign up or get in touch. The final submit is blocked, so he stops at the form.

## Goal

"Reach the form and fill it in on a phone without losing his place."

## Steps Eli would take

1. Find the sign-up or contact link.
2. Open the form and check the keyboard type suits each field, such as a number pad for a phone number.
3. Fill the fields. Check labels stay visible while he types.
4. Scroll to the submit button and check it is reachable. Do not submit.
5. Finish.

## Success looks like

- The form fitted the screen with no sideways scroll.
- Each field was easy to tap and to read.

## What to report

Fields that were hard to use. Labels that disappeared. The number of screens the form took.
