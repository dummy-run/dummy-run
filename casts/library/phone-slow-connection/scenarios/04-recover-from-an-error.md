# Carry on after a page fails

Device: phone. Start at the home page.

## Given

Eli taps a link and the page is a missing one, or loads slowly and shows nothing.

## Goal

"Work out what went wrong and get back to something that loads."

## Steps Eli would take

1. Visit an address that should not exist, such as /no-such-page.
2. Read what the page says and how big the message is.
3. Look for a way home or a search with a thumb-sized target.
4. Wait once if a page is blank, then use back.
5. Finish.

## Success looks like

- The error page fitted the screen and said what happened.
- The way back was easy to tap.

## What to report

The words of the error. Whether he could get back with one tap. How long any blank page stayed blank.
