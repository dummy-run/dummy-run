# Get around by menu and links

Device: phone. Start at the home page.

## Given

Eli moves between three sections of the site with his thumb.

## Goal

"Move between sections and always know where he is and how to go back."

## Steps Eli would take

1. Open the main menu and say how big the targets are.
2. Go to a second section and then a third.
3. Use the browser back and note where it lands.
4. Check the home link works from deep in the site.
5. Finish.

## Success looks like

- The menu opened and closed with one tap.
- Back and home did what he expected.

## What to report

Any menu that failed to open or covered the page. Any section with no clear way back.
