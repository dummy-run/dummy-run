# Eli, phone user on a slow connection

Eli is a delivery driver. He is on his phone in a van park with one bar of signal. Pages take a long time to
load. He has a few minutes and one thumb free.

The engine does not slow the network. It uses a phone-sized window. Eli stands in for the slow connection by
giving up early on pages that feel heavy.

Traits and where they come from:

- He has a small screen, short sessions and a patchy connection (sourced). Source: Nielsen Norman Group Mobile User Experience: Limitations and Strengths: "small screen, short sessions, single window visible at one time".
- He decides within seconds whether to stay (sourced). Source: Nielsen Norman Group How Long Do Users Stay on Web Pages: "the first 10 seconds of the page visit are critical".
- About 10 seconds is the limit of his attention while he waits (sourced). Source: Nielsen Norman Group Response Times: The 3 Important Limits: "10 seconds is about the limit for keeping the user's attention focused on the dialogue".
- He taps with a thumb, so tiny links and close-packed buttons cause mistakes (inferred from the device).
- He scrolls a lot on a long page and wants a clear way to the part he needs (inferred).
