# Find a fact by headings

Device: desktop. Start at the home page.

## Given

Noor wants one fact about [the product], for example what it costs or who to contact. She cannot scan the page by eye.

## Goal

"Find [the fact] using only the headings, links and labels, and say how many steps it took."

## Steps Noor would take

1. Read the page title and the main heading.
2. List the headings in order and judge whether the outline makes sense.
3. Follow the heading or link that best matches the fact.
4. Read the answer and note whether she knew where she was throughout.
5. Run `a11y` on the page where she found it. Finish.

## Success looks like

- She found the fact through headings and links alone.
- Every heading she used described its section.

## What to report

The path as step numbers. Headings that were missing, skipped a level or said nothing. The `a11y` rule IDs.
