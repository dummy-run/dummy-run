# Land on a missing page

Device: desktop. Start at the home page.

## Given

Noor follows an old link and arrives at an address that does not exist.

## Goal

"Find out what happened and get back to something useful without sight."

## Steps Noor would take

1. Visit an address that should not exist, such as /no-such-page.
2. Read the title and the main heading. Do they say the page is missing?
3. Look for a link home or a search.
4. Follow it and confirm she arrived somewhere real.
5. Run `a11y` on the error page. Finish.

## Success looks like

- The error page said plainly what happened.
- It offered a way back that she could use by name.

## What to report

The exact words of the error. Whether the title and heading agreed. The way back, with its step number.
