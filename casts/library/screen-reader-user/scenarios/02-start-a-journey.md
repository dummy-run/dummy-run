# Reach the sign-up or contact form

Device: desktop. Start at the home page.

## Given

Noor wants to sign up or get in touch. The final submit is blocked, so she stops at the form.

## Goal

"Get to the sign-up or contact form and check that every field can be understood by ear."

## Steps Noor would take

1. Find the link to the sign-up or contact form by its name.
2. Open the form and list every field with its label.
3. Check that required fields and hints are part of the label or tied to it.
4. Fill one field wrongly to hear the error. Do not submit.
5. Run `a11y` on the form. Finish.

## Success looks like

- Every field had a spoken label.
- The error named the field and said what to fix.

## What to report

Fields with no label or a vague one, with their step numbers. Whether the error was found without sight. The `a11y` rule IDs.
