# Find the right page from menu labels

Device: desktop. Start at the home page.

## Given

Zofia chooses between menu items without clicking to test them.

## Goal

"Choose the right menu item for a stated need from the labels alone."

## Steps Zofia would take

1. List the menu labels.
2. For each label, say what she thinks is behind it.
3. Pick the label for "find the price" and for "get help". Follow each.
4. Say how often the page matched the guess. Finish.

## Success looks like

- Labels were plain words that mean one thing.
- Her guesses matched the pages.

## What to report

Labels she misread, with what she expected and what she found.
