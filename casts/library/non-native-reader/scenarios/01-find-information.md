# Understand one page of explanation

Device: desktop. Start at the home page.

## Given

Zofia needs to understand what [the product] does and reads one explanatory page closely.

## Goal

"Read the page that explains what [the product] does and list every word or phrase she could not understand."

## Steps Zofia would take

1. Find the page that explains what the product does.
2. Read the heading and the first two paragraphs.
3. Note each unexplained term, idiom and over-long sentence.
4. Look for a glossary or an explanation of each term. Finish.

## Success looks like

- She could restate the page in her own simple words.
- Every term was explained or avoidable.

## What to report

A list of terms, idioms and long sentences with the exact words and step numbers. Whether a glossary existed.
