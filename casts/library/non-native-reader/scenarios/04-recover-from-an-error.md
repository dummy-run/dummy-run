# Understand an error message

Device: desktop. Start at the home page.

## Given

Zofia meets a page that does not exist, or an error in a field.

## Goal

"Understand what went wrong and what to do next."

## Steps Zofia would take

1. Visit an address that should not exist, such as /no-such-page.
2. Read the message. Mark any word or phrase she cannot use.
3. Find the next step.
4. Finish.

## Success looks like

- The message used plain words and said what to do.
- She did not need a translation tool.

## What to report

The exact words of the message and what confused her.
