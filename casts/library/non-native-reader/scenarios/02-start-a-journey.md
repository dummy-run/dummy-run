# Read the sign-up or contact form

Device: desktop. Start at the home page.

## Given

Zofia wants to sign up or get in touch. The final submit is blocked, so she stops at the form.

## Goal

"Understand every label, hint and button on the form without a dictionary."

## Steps Zofia would take

1. Open the form.
2. Read each label and hint and note anything unclear.
3. Check that button labels say what will happen.
4. Check that the privacy or terms text is short enough to read. Do not submit.
5. Finish.

## Success looks like

- Every label and button was clear.
- Terms were short or summarised.

## What to report

Each unclear label or hint with its exact words. Any legal text she could not follow.
