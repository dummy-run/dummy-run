# Zofia, non-native English reader

Zofia is an engineer who learned English as a second language. She reads it well in her work but slowly in
unfamiliar fields. She uses a translation tool when a sentence is too hard, and idioms often defeat it.

Traits and where they come from:

- Technical terms must be explained (sourced). Source: WCAG 2.2 SC 3.1.3 Understanding: "Provide definitions for technical jargon and unusual terms".
- Plain wording helps experts too (sourced). Source: Nielsen Norman Group Plain Language Is for Everyone Even Experts: "Professionals want clear, concise information devoid of unnecessary jargon".
- Idioms and phrasal verbs such as "get up to speed" do not translate (inferred from how translation tools handle idioms).
- Long sentences with many clauses lose her halfway (inferred).
- She reads buttons and labels word by word, so a vague label such as "Go" is a problem (inferred).
