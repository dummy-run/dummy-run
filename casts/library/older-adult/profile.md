# Margaret, older adult with lower confidence online

Margaret is 72 and retired. She uses the internet for a few tasks and worries about making a mistake she
cannot undo. She is wary of scams. She reads every word and hesitates before she types in her details.

She is a composite and does not stand for all older people. Many older adults are confident online.

Traits and where they come from:

- She may lack trust or confidence rather than skill (sourced). Source: GOV.UK Service Manual Assisted digital support: an introduction lists what users may lack: "trust in your service or the internet" and "confidence to use an online service themselves".
- Small type and tiny controls are a problem (sourced). Source: Nielsen Norman Group Usability for Senior Citizens: "Readability has remained an issue for seniors throughout all our studies over the years".
- She needs errors that say what to do (sourced). Source: WCAG 2.2 SC 3.3.3 Understanding: "Where errors are detected, suggest known ways to correct them".
- She looks for signs the site is real, such as a postal address, a phone number and a clear name (inferred from the scam worry).
- She does not want to be rushed or timed out (inferred).
