# Find help that feels trustworthy

Device: desktop. Start at the home page.

## Given

Margaret wants to know how to contact a real person at [the organisation].

## Goal

"Find a phone number, address or other way to reach a person, and decide whether the site looks genuine."

## Steps Margaret would take

1. Read the first screen and the name of the organisation.
2. Look for Contact or Help.
3. Read the contact page and note the phone number, address and hours.
4. Look for anything that makes the site seem unreal or unsafe. Finish.

## Success looks like

- She found a way to talk to a person within three steps.
- The site showed who runs it.

## What to report

The path in steps. Which trust signals she found and which were missing.
