# Recover from a mistake

Device: desktop. Start at the home page.

## Given

Margaret types something wrong in a field, or follows a bad link.

## Goal

"Find out what went wrong and fix it without fear."

## Steps Margaret would take

1. Enter a clearly wrong value in a form field, such as letters in a phone number.
2. Read the message. Note whether it blames her or helps her.
3. Correct the field. Do not submit.
4. Visit an address that should not exist, such as /no-such-page, and read that message too.
5. Finish.

## Success looks like

- The messages said plainly what to do.
- She felt she could fix it herself.

## What to report

The words of each message. Whether it reassured her or alarmed her.
