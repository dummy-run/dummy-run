# Reach the sign-up or contact form with confidence

Device: desktop. Start at the home page.

## Given

Margaret wants to sign up or get in touch. The final submit is blocked, so she stops at the form.

## Goal

"Reach the form and say whether she would feel safe filling it in."

## Steps Margaret would take

1. Find the link to the form.
2. Read what the page says about why it needs her details and what happens next.
3. Read every label. Note any she does not understand.
4. Look for a way to stop or go back without losing anything. Do not submit.
5. Finish.

## Success looks like

- The form said why it needed each detail.
- Margaret knew what would happen after she pressed the button.

## What to report

Anything she would not fill in and why. Labels that worried her, with step numbers.
