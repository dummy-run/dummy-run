# Find the way back

Device: desktop. Start at the home page.

## Given

Margaret goes two or three pages deep and then wants to start again.

## Goal

"Return to the home page and to the previous page without getting lost."

## Steps Margaret would take

1. Follow three links into the site.
2. Use the browser back button and note where she lands.
3. Find the home link or the logo and use it.
4. Say whether she always knew where she was. Finish.

## Success looks like

- Back and home always did what she expected.
- The page title or heading told her where she was.

## What to report

Any step where she did not know where she was.
