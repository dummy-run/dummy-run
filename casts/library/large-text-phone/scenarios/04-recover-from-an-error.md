# Carry on after an error with larger text

Device: phone. Start at the home page.

## Given

Ivy reaches a missing page, or a form shows an error message.

## Goal

"Read the message and find the way back when everything is large."

## Steps Ivy would take

1. Visit an address that should not exist, such as /no-such-page.
2. Read what the page says. Say whether the message fitted the screen.
3. Look for a way home or a search, and say whether it is whole and easy to tap.
4. Use it to get back to a page that loads.
5. Finish.

## Success looks like

- The error message fitted the screen and was fully readable.
- The way back was not cut off or covered.

## What to report

The words of the error. Anything that overlapped or ran off the screen. Whether she could get back with one tap.
