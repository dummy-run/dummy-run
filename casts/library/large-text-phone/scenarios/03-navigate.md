# Get around by menu and links with larger text

Device: phone. Start at the home page.

## Given

Ivy moves between three sections of the site with her thumb.

## Goal

"Move between sections and always read the menu, the page title and the way back."

## Steps Ivy would take

1. Open the main menu and read every item.
2. Go to a second section and then a third.
3. Check the header and any fixed bar. Say how much of the screen they take.
4. Use back and the home link.
5. Finish.

## Success looks like

- The menu opened and every item fitted.
- A fixed bar left room to read the page.

## What to report

A menu with overlapping or cut-off items. A fixed bar that covered content. Any control she could not read in full.
