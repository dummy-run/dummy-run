# Use the sign-up or contact form with larger text

Device: phone. Start at the home page.

## Given

Ivy wants to sign up or get in touch. The final submit is blocked, so she stops at the form.

## Goal

"Fill in the form at 200 percent text and see every label, hint and error."

## Steps Ivy would take

1. Find the sign-up or contact link and open the form.
2. Read each label. Say whether any label is cut off or sits on top of its field.
3. Type into the fields and check the text stays inside them.
4. Scroll to the submit button and check it is whole and not covered. Do not submit.
5. Finish.

## Success looks like

- Every label, hint and button was fully readable.
- No field or button overlapped another.

## What to report

Labels or buttons that were cut off or overlapped. Fields where the typed text ran out of the box.
