# Look for a record of admin actions

Device: desktop. Start at the home page.

## Given

Ada has just invited a user and changed a role. Use a test environment with test data and the test account for this role. The target must allow changes.

## Goal

"Find a log that shows who did what and when."

## Steps Ada would take

1. Look for Activity, Audit or History in the menu.
2. Open it.
3. Look for her invite and role change.
4. Check it shows who, what and when. Check a viewer could not edit it.
5. Finish.

## Success looks like

- Her actions appeared with who, what and when.
- The log could not be changed from the page.

## What to report

Whether each action was logged, with step numbers. What the log lacked.
