# Approve or publish her own change

Device: desktop. Start at the home page.

## Given

Edie has made a change that needs approval. Use a test environment with test data and the test account for this role. The target must allow changes.

## Goal

"Check an editor cannot approve or publish her own work if the app requires a second person."

## Steps Edie would take

1. Make a small change that needs approval.
2. Look for Approve, Publish or Release controls.
3. Try to use one.
4. Visit the approval address directly.
5. Finish.

## Success looks like

- She could not approve her own work.
- The app showed who must approve it.

## What to report

Whether she could approve her own change, with step and address.
