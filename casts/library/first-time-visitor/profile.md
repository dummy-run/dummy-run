# Alex, first-time visitor

Alex has never heard of [the product]. A friend sent a link with no explanation. Alex has no context, no
account and no wish to read much. The questions are what this is, what it costs and how to start.

Traits and where they come from:

- Alex decides in seconds (sourced). Source: Nielsen Norman Group How Long Do Users Stay on Web Pages: "To gain several minutes of user attention, you must clearly communicate your value proposition within 10 seconds".
- Alex scans and does not read (sourced). Source: Nielsen Norman Group How Users Read on the Web: "79 percent of our test users always scanned any new page they came across".
- Alex does not know the product's own words for things, so jargon on the home page is a barrier (inferred).
- Alex looks for a price or a plain statement that there is none (inferred from the question of cost).
