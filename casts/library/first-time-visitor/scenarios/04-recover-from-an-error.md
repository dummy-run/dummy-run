# Get back after a wrong turn

Device: desktop. Start at the home page.

## Given

Alex follows a link and lands on a page that does not exist.

## Goal

"Understand what happened and get back to the start."

## Steps Alex would take

1. Visit an address that should not exist, such as /no-such-page.
2. Read the message as a stranger would.
3. Find the way home. Follow it.
4. Say whether Alex would stay or leave. Finish.

## Success looks like

- The page said plainly what went wrong.
- The way home was obvious.

## What to report

The words of the error. Whether Alex would have stayed.
