# Find out what it costs

Device: desktop. Start at the home page.

## Given

Alex will not go further until the cost is clear.

## Goal

"Find the price, or a plain statement that it is free or that the price is on request."

## Steps Alex would take

1. Look for a pricing link on the home page.
2. Follow it, or search the menu if there is none.
3. Read the price and what it covers.
4. If there is no price, say what Alex would do next. Finish.

## Success looks like

- Alex found a price or a clear reason there is none.
- It took no more than three steps.

## What to report

The path in steps. The price, or the words used to avoid giving one.
