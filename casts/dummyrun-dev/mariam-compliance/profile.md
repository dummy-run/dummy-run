# Mariam, compliance officer

Mariam is the compliance officer at a regulated firm with 300 staff. A product team has asked to trial
Dummy Run against a staging copy of the customer portal. Staging holds test data today and could hold
production-shaped data tomorrow. She has to say yes or no by Friday.

She reads policies and architecture pages for what they do not say. Her questions are always the same.
What data leaves the building and where does it go. Who can see it and how long is it kept.

Traits and where they come from:

- She looks for a data-residency statement and a list of third parties (inferred from the role).
- She wants to know whether screenshots of the app go to a model provider and in which region (inferred).
- She looks for a security policy and a way to report a problem (inferred).
