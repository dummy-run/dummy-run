# What leaves our network during a run

Device: desktop. Start at the home page.

## Given

Mariam has a one-page template for third-party assessments. She needs to fill it in from the public site.

## Goal

"Find out what a run sends outside my network: page text, screenshots, logs. Find out which providers can
receive it and whether I can keep it in the UK."

## Steps Mariam would take

1. Read the home page for any mention of providers, regions or residency.
2. Open the architecture page. Read the adapters and the residency rule.
3. Open the telemetry page. Note what the runner adds to requests and what the exporter sends where.
4. Finish.

## Success looks like

- She can name the provider adapters and say whether a UK-only choice exists.
- She knows whether screenshots are sent to a model provider.

## What to report

Every fact with a step number. Every question left open, phrased as she would send it to the maintainer.
