# How security problems are reported

Device: desktop. Start at the home page.

## Given

Mariam's checklist asks whether the vendor has a disclosure policy and a response time.

## Goal

"Find the security policy. Find how to report a vulnerability and how long they take to reply."

## Steps Mariam would take

1. Look for a security link on the home page, in the footer or in the documentation list.
2. Read the policy. Note the contact route and any stated response time.
3. Look for what happens to findings about apps the maintainers do not own.
4. Finish.

## Success looks like

- She found a disclosure route and a response time, or found that one is missing.

## What to report

What the policy says, with step numbers. Whether it was findable in three steps. What is missing.
