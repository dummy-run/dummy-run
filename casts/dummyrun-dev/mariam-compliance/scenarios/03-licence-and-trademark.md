# Licence and trademark terms

Device: desktop. Start at the home page.

## Given

Legal has asked Mariam whether the firm may run the tool internally and whether it may say so in a tender.

## Goal

"Find the licence. Find what the trademark policy allows the firm to say."

## Steps Mariam would take

1. Find the licence from the home page.
2. Find the trademark or brand policy.
3. Read what each allows and forbids.
4. Finish.

## Success looks like

- She can name the licence and state one thing the trademark policy forbids.

## What to report

The licence name and where it is stated. The trademark rules she found. Anything legal would still ask.
