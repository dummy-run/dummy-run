# Does it replace my scripted suite

Device: desktop. Start at the home page.

## Given

Priya has ten minutes between meetings. She has heard the name and nothing else.

## Goal

"Work out in ten minutes what Dummy Run does, how a finding is checked before anyone sees it, and whether it
could take over the exploratory testing my team does by hand."

## Steps Priya would take

1. Read the home page from top to bottom.
2. Follow the link that explains how it works or the architecture.
3. Look for the evidence rule: what happens to a claim with no screenshot or log.
4. Look for which browsers are supported.
5. Finish with a verdict on whether she would book a trial.

## Success looks like

- She can say in one sentence what the tool does.
- She found how a finding is verified and who decides it becomes a ticket.
- She found the supported browsers.

## What to report

Each place she looked and what it said, with step numbers. Anything she expected and did not find.
