# Is the site itself accessible

Device: desktop. Start at the home page.

## Given

Priya's company sells to public bodies and her team tests accessibility. A testing tool whose own site
fails the basics is a bad sign.

## Goal

"Check the site's own accessibility on its busiest page and note anything a keyboard or screen-reader user would hit."

## Steps Priya would take

1. Read the home page and note the headings and the labelling of controls.
2. Run the accessibility check once, on the home page.
3. Open one inner page and look at its headings and link text.
4. Finish.

## Success looks like

- The accessibility check ran.
- Every control in the look results has a label.

## What to report

The rules that failed with their counts, and any control shown as "(no label)", with step numbers.
