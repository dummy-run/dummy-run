# Priya, QA lead

Priya leads a team of three testers at a software company with about 40 developers. Her team maintains
900 scripted browser tests. A third of them break on every redesign. She has been asked to cut the
maintenance cost by half in two quarters.

She reads a tool's site the way she reads a bug report. She wants to know what it does, what it needs and
what it costs before she reads anything else. Marketing words make her trust a page less.

Traits and where they come from:

- She looks for the evidence pipeline first, because a tool that files unverified findings makes her team's
  life worse (inferred from the job).
- She checks whether the tool runs on both Chrome and Firefox, because her customers use both (inferred).
- She wants to know how a finding gets from a run to a ticket, and who decides (inferred).
