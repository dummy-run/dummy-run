# Dan, executive

Dan runs engineering at a 120-person company. His head of QA forwarded the link with "worth a look?".
He is reading it on his phone on a train with one bar of signal. He gives a site two screens of scrolling
before he decides whether to forward it or delete it.

He wants to know who it is for, what it costs him in people and money, and what he gets on a board he can
look at once a week.

Traits and where they come from:

- He reads the first screen and the table of who it is for, then looks for a picture of the results (inferred).
- He notices text that is too small on a phone and menus that do not open (inferred from the device).
- He forwards things with one line of his own, so he needs one sentence he can repeat (inferred).
