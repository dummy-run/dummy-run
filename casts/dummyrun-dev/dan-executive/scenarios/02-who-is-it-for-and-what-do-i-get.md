# Who is it for and what do I get

Device: phone. Start at the home page.

## Given

Dan's head of QA will ask him what the board shows and who would own the tool.

## Goal

"Find the table of who it is for and the description of the results board. Decide which of my teams would own it."

## Steps Dan would take

1. Find the section that says who the tool is for.
2. Read the row that matches an executive and the row for QA.
3. Look for what the results board shows and who decides what becomes a ticket.
4. Finish.

## Success looks like

- He can say which team would own the tool and what he would see each week.

## What to report

What the rows said, with steps. Whether the picture of the results was clear on a phone. The question he
would ask before paying for anything.
