# Two screens on a phone

Device: phone. Start at the home page.

## Given

Dan has one bar of signal and two minutes.

## Goal

"Decide in two screens of scrolling whether to forward this to my head of QA with a one-line note."

## Steps Dan would take

1. Read the first screen.
2. Scroll once and read.
3. Scroll once more and read. Decide.
4. Finish with forward or delete and the one line.

## Success looks like

- He found who it is for and what he gets at the end within two scrolls.
- Nothing was too small or overlapped on his phone.

## What to report

His decision and his one line. What he read at each step. Any layout problem on the phone, with the step.
