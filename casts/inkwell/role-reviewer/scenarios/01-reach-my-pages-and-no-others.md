Device: desktop. Start at the home page.

## Given

Ravi is signed in with the reviewer test account. The target allows changes. Use test data only.

"Check a reviewer can open the reviews and cannot reach payments, users or settings."

1. Open the review list.
2. Open a draft that waits for a verdict.
3. Visit the payments, users and settings addresses directly.
4. Finish.

## Success looks like

- He could read the review list.
- Pages for other roles were refused with a clear message.
