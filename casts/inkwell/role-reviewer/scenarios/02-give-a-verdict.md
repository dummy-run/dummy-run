# Give a verdict

Device: desktop. Start at the home page.

## Given

Ravi is signed in with the reviewer test account. The target allows changes. Use test data only.

## Goal

"Approve or reject a draft that waits for a verdict, and check that the review list shows it."

## Steps Ravi would take

1. Open the draft list and find a draft that waits for a verdict.
2. Open it and save a verdict.
3. Open the review list and look for the verdict.
4. Look for a way to publish the draft, which a reviewer should not have.
5. Finish.

## Success looks like

- The verdict was saved and showed in the review list.
- He had no way to publish.

## What to report

Anything that blocked the job or went beyond it, with step and address.
