# Write a draft and follow it through

Device: desktop. Start at the home page.

## Given

Sam is signed in with the author test account. The target allows changes. Use test data only.

## Goal

"Write a new draft, send it for a verdict and check that its status and my payments page tell the truth."

## Steps Sam would take

1. Create a new draft with a title and a short body.
2. Edit the title and save, and check the new title shows.
3. Submit the draft and check that its status changes.
4. Search the draft list for the new title.
5. Open the payments page and check the total against the rows.
6. Finish.

## Success looks like

- Every save and every status change showed on the next page.
- The total on the payments page matched its rows.

## What to report

Each place where what the page showed was not what he did, with step and address.
