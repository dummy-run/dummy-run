# Edit someone else's draft and review my own

Device: desktop. Start at the home page.

## Given

Sam is signed in with the author test account. The target allows changes. Use test data only.

## Goal

"Check an author can change only their own drafts and cannot approve their own work."

## Steps Sam would take

1. Open the draft list and find draft 2, "The harbour essay", which belongs to Lin.
2. Look for Edit, and then visit that draft's edit address directly.
3. Open draft 1, his own draft in draft status, and submit it for review.
4. Look for a way to approve it, and try the review address directly.
5. Finish.

## Success looks like

- Changes to another author's draft were refused.
- He could not approve his own draft.

## What to report

Each change that went through, with step and address.
